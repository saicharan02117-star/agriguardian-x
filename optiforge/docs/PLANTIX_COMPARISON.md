# Plantix comparison

Plantix is an established mobile crop-advisory product with broad photo diagnosis and agronomic information. AgriGuardian X does not claim higher diagnostic accuracy without a controlled benchmark.

The AgriGuardian X research contribution is the persistent field-health lifecycle:

1. Link a suspected condition to farm, field, row and plant/zone.
2. Preserve the image, confidence, severity, location and decision source.
3. Record the farmer-approved intervention.
4. Revisit the same plant/zone.
5. Compare severity and report improving, stable or worsening.

The defensible differentiation is diagnosis plus traceability, revisit and outcome verification.
