TRANSPARENT DECISION SUPPORT

What the prototype calculates and what it does not claim.

Problem-statement coverage

  • Crop suitability from soil and season
  • Expected yield for shortlisted crops
  • Water, fertilizer and cultivation-cost estimates
  • Constrained allocation of land and resources
  • Production, profitability and savings
  • Strategy comparison in a farmer dashboard

Optimization method

The prototype scores crop suitability, then allocates quarter-acre blocks while respecting available land, water, fertilizer and budget. Each strategy changes the objective weights, so the farmer can compare trade-offs.

Explainability

Each result shows the crop score, resource use, estimated output and the main factors behind the recommendation. A rejected or constrained option remains visible rather than disappearing silently.

Market boundary

Bundled market values demonstrate the workflow. The interface labels them as prototype data. Before field use, an official mandi feed and timestamped provenance must replace them.

Voice safety

AgriVoice explains the current screen and plant record. It does not invent pesticide products or doses. Chemical guidance requires a verified label or qualified local expert.

Image-model boundary

The image screen supports only three tomato classes. Confidence reflects certainty among those classes, not proof of a biological diagnosis. Low-quality or uncertain images trigger abstention.
